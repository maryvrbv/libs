# Why Polish Matters

AI-generated UI optimizes for one thing: working. Claude Code will give you functional layouts, correct component usage, and sensible defaults. But "works" and "feels right" are different standards. Your users can tell the difference in under a second.

The gap between a hackathon project and a shipped product isn't complexity. It's refinement. And it comes down to three things.

## The Big Three

**Spacing.** AI output uses inconsistent gaps. A 12px margin here, a 20px padding there, a 15px gap somewhere else. Your eye registers this as chaos even if your brain can't name it.

**Typography.** Font sizes that don't follow a scale. Line heights that are too tight or too loose. Headings that don't feel different enough from body text. The hierarchy is flat, so nothing guides the eye.

**Color consistency.** Too many grays. Borders and backgrounds that almost match but don't. Text colors that shift slightly between sections. The palette feels accidental.

Fix these three and you fix 80% of what makes AI output look unfinished.

## The Squint Test

Blur your eyes or step back from the screen. What you see is pure visual hierarchy — shape, contrast, weight. If everything looks the same importance, your layout has no hierarchy. If your eye lands somewhere specific and then flows naturally through the content, you're in good shape.

Do this before and after every polish pass. It's the fastest gut check you have.

## What Polish Is Not

Polish is not redesigning. You are not starting over. You are not rethinking the layout, swapping components, or reimagining the UX.

Polish is refinement. You take what Claude Code built — the structure, the components, the flow — and you tighten it. Consistent spacing. Intentional typography. A coherent color palette. Same design, elevated execution.

That's the work. Let's get into it.
