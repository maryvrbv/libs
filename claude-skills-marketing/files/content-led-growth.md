## Skill: Content-Led Growth

A content-led growth strategy defines how content becomes your primary acquisition engine — not a support function, but the main way people discover, trust, and buy your product. Marketers write this to connect content creation directly to revenue through search, distribution, and conversion path design. It answers: "How does every piece of content we create move someone from stranger to customer — and how do we measure that?"

### How to Write Each Section

**Content-Market Fit**
This is the most important section. If you write great content for the wrong audience, it doesn't matter. Define who you're writing for with precision — not "marketers" but "B2B SaaS marketing managers who manage paid acquisition budgets of $50K+/month and need to prove ROI to their VP." Then validate that your content resonates with signals: conversion rates, engagement metrics, and qualitative feedback (do readers share it, quote it, send it to colleagues?).

Good: "Our primary persona is engineering managers at mid-market companies (100-1,000 employees) who are evaluating CI/CD tools. They search for comparison content ('Jenkins vs GitHub Actions'), best practice guides ('how to set up deployment pipelines'), and templates ('CI/CD configuration templates'). Our content POV: we've run production deployments at scale and write from operator experience, not vendor marketing."

Bad: "We write for developers who want to learn." (Too broad, no search behavior, no content angle.)

**SEO + Content Flywheel**
Map how content feeds itself. The flywheel is: create content → rank → traffic → conversions → happy customers → backlinks → higher authority → better rankings → more traffic. Each part of the flywheel should be actively managed. Content without distribution doesn't get traffic. Traffic without CTAs doesn't get conversions. Conversions without a good product don't get word-of-mouth. A break in any link stops the flywheel.

**Conversion Path Design**
Every piece of content needs a clear next step for the reader. TOFU content should capture email or offer a lead magnet. MOFU content should drive to product pages or demos. BOFU content should have direct signup CTAs. Map every conversion path explicitly — where does the reader enter, what do they read, where is the CTA, what happens after they click? If you can't draw the path from blog post to paying customer, the content is entertainment, not a growth engine.

**Content Operations**
Content-led growth fails on operations more often than strategy. You need a sustainable publishing cadence, a clear workflow from brief to published, and quality standards that prevent filler content from diluting your brand. Set realistic production timelines — a 2,500-word blog post takes 3-5 days of work, not 2 hours. Plan capacity before committing to frequency.

**Measurement Framework**
Measure content at three levels: (1) per-post performance (did this specific piece work?), (2) cluster performance (is this topic driving traffic and conversions?), (3) program performance (is content-led growth delivering on its promise?). Use first-touch attribution to understand what content attracts, last-touch to understand what converts, and multi-touch to understand the full journey.

### Conventions

- Length: 5-8 pages
- Tone: Strategic, data-driven, focused on the connection between content and revenue
- Every piece of content must map to a keyword (TOFU/MOFU) or a conversion goal (BOFU)
- Set publishing cadence based on realistic production capacity — consistency beats volume
- Repurpose every long-form piece into 5+ distribution assets
- Include a CTA in every piece of content — no exceptions
- Track content-attributed revenue, not just traffic
- Refresh existing content before creating new content on the same topic
- Internal linking is mandatory — every new post links to 3+ existing posts and vice versa
- Content quality > content quantity. One great post outperforms five mediocre ones
- Attribution matters — set up first-touch and last-touch tracking before publishing at scale
- Content strategy should inform product roadmap — what people search for reveals what they need

### Anti-Patterns

- Do NOT create content without keyword research — "interesting topics" that nobody searches for don't drive organic growth
- Do NOT write for search engines at the expense of humans — content that ranks but reads like SEO filler won't convert
- Do NOT skip the conversion path — blog posts without CTAs are content marketing, not content-led growth
- Do NOT publish inconsistently — a blog that posts 8 articles in one week then goes silent for a month builds no audience
- Do NOT ignore content refresh — a 2-year-old post with outdated statistics actively damages your credibility
- Do NOT measure content success only by traffic — 10,000 visitors who don't convert are less valuable than 500 who do
- Do NOT create thin content to fill a publishing calendar — Google's Helpful Content Update penalizes sites with low-quality filler
- Do NOT duplicate topics across multiple posts — consolidate related content into comprehensive pillar pages
- Do NOT neglect distribution — "publish and pray" doesn't work. 50% of the effort should be distribution, not just creation
- Do NOT write without a content brief — unstructured writing leads to unfocused posts that rank for nothing
- Do NOT treat content as a siloed function — content strategy should align with product, sales, and customer success
- Do NOT expect results in weeks — content-led growth compounds over 6-12 months. Impatience kills programs that would have worked
- Do NOT create content that exists elsewhere in the same form — your unique angle, data, or experience is what makes it worth reading
- Do NOT forget about existing customers — content for retention and expansion is just as valuable as acquisition content

### Content Production Workflow
1. **Week 1 planning (monthly):** Review keyword targets, prioritize topics, assign briefs
2. **Brief creation (1 day):** Target keyword, outline, CTA strategy, internal links, lead magnet, competitor benchmarks
3. **Draft (2-3 days):** Writer produces first draft following brief and quality standards
4. **Edit (1 day):** Mira reviews for clarity, accuracy, SEO optimization (Clearscope score 80+), and conversion
5. **Design (1 day):** Custom OG image, 2-3 inline images/diagrams, alt text
6. **Publish + distribute (1 day):** CMS publish, email to list, social posts queued, Reddit answers drafted
7. **7-day review:** Check traffic, rankings, engagement, CTA clicks. Adjust if underperforming
8. **30-day review:** Full performance assessment. Refresh or promote further if strong

### Content Quality Standards
- Every post answers a specific question or solves a specific problem — no "roundup" posts
- Minimum 1 original framework, data point, or opinion per post — never a rewrite of page-one content
- "What actually happened" section in every TOFU/MOFU post — show real screenshots, real data
- Internal links: minimum 4 per post (1 to pillar, 1 to product, 2 to related posts)
- External links: 2-3 authoritative sources per post
- Clearscope optimization score: 80+ before publish
- Reading level: Grade 8-10 (Hemingway App check)
- No stock photos — custom screenshots, diagrams, or illustrations only
