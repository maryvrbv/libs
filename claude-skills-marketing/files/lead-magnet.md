### How to Write Each Section

**Asset Strategy:** The lead magnet must solve a real, specific problem — not vaguely educate. The "Lead-to-Product Bridge" is the most important strategic element: how does using this free asset naturally create awareness of or desire for the paid product? If there is no bridge, the lead magnet generates emails that never convert.

Good bridge: "The calculator shows you exactly how much revenue you're losing to churn. Once they see the number, they want to fix it — which is what our product does."

Bad bridge: "We'll collect their email and add them to our nurture sequence." (This is a tactic, not a bridge.)

**Asset Outline:** The asset must deliver value in under 10 minutes. If it takes 30 minutes to use, most people will not complete it. Design for quick wins — the user should get a result, an insight, or a usable output within minutes of opening. Structure the content as actions, not reading.

Good: "Section 1: Input your monthly revenue, churn rate, and CAC. The calculator instantly shows your Customer Lifetime Value and how much each 1% churn reduction is worth in annual revenue."

Bad: "Section 1: An introduction to the concept of churn and why it matters for SaaS businesses."

**Landing Page Copy:** The headline states the outcome, not the asset. "Calculate exactly how much churn is costing you" is better than "Download our Churn Calculator." Form fields: fewer is better. Email-only gets the highest conversion rate. Every additional field reduces conversions by 10-25%.

**Email Promotion:** The delivery email is transactional — deliver the asset immediately, add one quick-start tip, and sign off. Do not sell in the delivery email. The Day 3 follow-up re-engages people who downloaded but did not use it. The Day 7 nurture introduces the product connection softly.

**Distribution Plan:** A lead magnet without distribution is a file on a server. Plan at least 3 channels before production begins. The highest-converting channel for most lead magnets is a blog post optimized for the keyword the audience is searching when they have the problem the asset solves.

### Conventions

- The asset delivers a specific, usable result within 10 minutes. Not education — output.
- Landing page form asks for the minimum fields needed. Email-only is the default. Add fields only if you need them for segmentation or lead scoring.
- The headline on the landing page describes the outcome, not the format. "See how much churn costs you" not "Download our PDF."
- Delivery email sends immediately — not "within 24 hours." Immediate delivery converts at 2-3x the rate of delayed delivery.
- Every lead magnet has a clear Lead-to-Product Bridge documented in the strategy. If the asset does not connect to the product, it generates unqualified leads.
- The asset is branded but not promotional. Logo and colors yes. Product pitch inside the asset no.
- The landing page includes social proof — download count, testimonials, or logo bar.
- The distribution plan includes at least 3 channels and is written before the asset is produced.
- File format matches the audience's workflow. Marketers prefer Google Sheets. Developers prefer GitHub repos. Designers prefer Figma files. Executives prefer PDFs.
- Follow-up emails do not sell aggressively. Day 3: re-engage. Day 7: educate. Day 14+: soft product introduction.
- Conversion rate benchmark: 20-40% for landing pages with email-only forms. Below 20% means the headline or asset promise is weak.

### Anti-Patterns

- Do NOT create lead magnets that are thinly disguised product demos. "Download our free guide" that is 80% screenshots of your product is not a lead magnet — it is a brochure.
- Do NOT gate content that is freely available elsewhere. If someone can find the same information in a blog post or on Wikipedia, gating it annoys people and damages trust.
- Do NOT ask for 8 form fields. Every field beyond email reduces conversion rate. If you need company size and job title, use enrichment tools after capture.
- Do NOT deliver the asset as an attachment in the email. Host it on a URL and link to it. Attachments get caught by spam filters and cannot be tracked.
- Do NOT create an asset without planning distribution first. Building first and promoting second guarantees the asset sits on a landing page with no traffic.
- Do NOT write a 40-page ebook as a lead magnet. Nobody reads 40-page ebooks. Create a 3-page calculator, a 1-page checklist, or a 5-slide framework. Shorter assets get completed and shared.
- Do NOT make the asset require your product to use. The lead magnet must be standalone. If it only works with your software, it is a product tutorial, not a lead magnet.
- Do NOT forget the follow-up sequence. Capturing an email without a follow-up plan means you paid for a lead you will never convert.
- Do NOT sell in the delivery email. The person just gave you their email. Deliver value first. Sell later.
- Do NOT skip the competitive analysis. If three competitors already offer a similar calculator, yours needs a clear differentiator — better inputs, better outputs, or better design.
- Do NOT create a lead magnet for a keyword nobody searches. Validate search volume for the problem the asset solves before building it.
- Do NOT neglect the "Who This Is For" section on the landing page. Self-qualification reduces unqualified downloads and improves lead quality.

## 1. Asset Strategy

**Problem This Solves:** Most SaaS leaders know their churn rate but have not calculated the actual dollar impact — lifetime revenue lost, CAC wasted on churned customers, and the compounding effect on growth rate. They underestimate churn because they see it as a percentage, not a dollar figure.

**Promise:** In 5 minutes, you will know exactly how much churn is costing you annually — broken down by lost revenue, wasted acquisition spend, and reduced growth rate.

**Time to Value:** 5 minutes — enter 6 inputs, get a full financial breakdown immediately.

**Lead-to-Product Bridge:** Once a founder sees that churn is costing them $380K/year, they want to fix it. The calculator's final section shows the revenue impact of reducing churn by 1%, 2%, and 5% — which is exactly what RetainIQ delivers. The asset creates the urgency; the product is the solution.

### Customization

Adjust the asset type based on your audience and the problem. Calculators work best for financial decisions (ROI, cost analysis). Checklists work for process-oriented problems (launch readiness, audit). Templates work for recurring tasks (email templates, SOW templates). Reports work for industry benchmarks (salary data, market sizing). For bottom-of-funnel lead magnets, increase form fields (add company and phone) and make the follow-up sequence more sales-oriented with a demo CTA by Day 3. For top-of-funnel, keep the form to email-only and extend the nurture sequence to 2-3 weeks before any product mention.
